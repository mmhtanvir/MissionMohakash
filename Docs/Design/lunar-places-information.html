Approximation: sin(elevation) = sinφ·sinδ + cosφ·cosδ·cos h on a smooth sphere with no terrain. Real illumination depends on latitude, local terrain and time; polar regions can have extended illumination on elevated rims and permanent shadow in craters, which this model cannot capture (use LOLA-based illumination maps via Moon Trek). The slider's time axis starts at local noon.

Why the bundled location data is mostly qualitative
Site-specific measurements require extracting products from instrument archives; guessing them would break scientific accuracy. The page therefore bundles only well-established qualitative facts plus clearly-labelled geometric models.
