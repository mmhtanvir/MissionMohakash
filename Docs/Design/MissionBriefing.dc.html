MISSION BRIEFING

MISSION 01
FIRST LIGHT [ MOON ]
Lunar south pole 72 mission hours

Astronaut, you have landed alone on a sunny ridge near the Moon's south pole. Set up the outpost, keep yourself alive, place a seismometer far from the base to listen for moonquakes, and survive until the ride home at hour 72. Everything you bring is limited, so plan ahead.

Mission plan

  1. 1Pack your rocket.
  2. 2Pick up the seismometer crate by the airlock.
  3. 3Carry it past the orange ring (80 m) and press E to set it up.
  4. 4Log a radiation reading at the post next to the habitat.
  5. Bonus: collect 2 rock samples.
  6. 6Plan your 72 hours after packing. Don't forget to sleep.
  7. 7Go inside, speed up time and keep power and air going until hour 72.

Key ideas

Solar power
Only works in sunlight, so the battery stores the extra.
Shadow
At hour 40 the sun disappears for 6 hours.
Oxygen
The oxygen maker turns water into air, but needs a lot of power.
Spacewalk (EVA)
Your suit's oxygen runs out, so stay inside the cyan ring.
Radiation
A solar storm comes around hour 22, so be inside then.
